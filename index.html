Privacy Policy — “Al-Qur'an Al-Kareem” by Optica

Last updated: July 2026

The Al-Qur'an Al-Kareem app by Optica (optica.net) fully respects your privacy.

Data we collect

None. We collect no data at all.

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • The app sends no data to any server.
  • Everything runs on your device; the app works fully offline after first load.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (theme, bookmarks, last page) are stored only on your device and never leave it. Erase them anytime from Settings → Reset settings.

Location

If prayer-times or Qibla features are enabled in the future, your location is used to compute times and direction on your device only, and is never sent anywhere.

Feedback

If you use the in-app feedback option, your device's Mail app opens so you can send your message to it@optica.net yourself. Nothing is collected automatically.
